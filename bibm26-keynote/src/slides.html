The question

A woman who no longer knows her children can still sing every verse of a hymn

Could we use that on purpose, the way we use a drug?

In forty minutes you will see why we cannot yet, and what would change it.

Three findings

Where this talk lands

  1. We can now read music out of the brain, and AI models of music predict the brain.
  2. Almost all of it is correlation, and in the clinic music helps only modestly.
  3. Both have one cause: we cannot vary the music. Generative models can.
Read them slowly. Finding 3 is the thesis, stated fully only in Act 4. These three lines return as the contributions on the last slide.

One study a biosignal lab could run within a year Planned

  1. Generate pairs that differ in one property only: predictability, style, tempo.
  2. Record EEG while people listen, including older adults and hearing aid users.
  3. Test whether the response tracks the property beyond acoustic features.
  4. Report the generator settings as the dose.
Emphasize step 2: the populations where clinical evidence is weakest.
